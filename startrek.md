## The Next-Generation
From this tasting menu: https://curiousflotsam.blogspot.com/2013/03/tng.html
 1x01 Encounter at Farpoint. The first episode, introducing the main characters, and Q.

 1x13 Datalore. Data has a twin. An evil twin. An android evil twin in space.
 (Continued in 6x26)

 1x15 11001001. A delightful episode with a most unusual alien species. Specially recommended for the computer nerd or geek in your life.

 2x03 Elementary, Dear Data. Data as Holmes faces off Moriarty.
 (Continued in 6x12)

 2x09 The Measure of a Man. How sentient must a machine be before it stops being property?

 2x19 Manhunt. Lwaxana Troi puts the moves on a supremely uncomfortable Picard.
 (See also 3x24)

 3x10 The Defector. If only for Picard’s endgame.

 3x15 Yesterday’s Enterprise. The Enterprise of an alternative timeline is sacrificed to re-instate the prime timeline.

 3x21 Hollow Pursuits. As virtual worlds become more realistic, immersing oneself more fully becomes ever more enticing, especially for those whose experiences in real-life socialisation have been less than stellar. Science fiction is at its best when it not only predicts scientific advances but ponders their effect on human lives.

 3x23 Sarek. Sarek is Spock’s father, a much-loved Trek character for his embodiment of all that is Vulcan. Sarek and Spock are Trek’s best known father and son, and this episode explores their complex relationship.

 3x24 Ménage à Troi. Picard resorts to reciting Shakespeare to rescue Lwaxana from her Ferengi abductor.

 4x11 Data’s Day. A day in the life of Cmdr. Data. He learns to dance, which is delightful.

 4x21 The Drumhead. Another High Trek episode, it deals with questions of loyalty and origin in the midst of paranoia, with some only too eager to sacrifice a great deal of freedom for a little security.

 4x25 In Theory. Data pursues a romantic relationship by the book.

 5x02 Darmok. One of Trek’s most celebrated episodes, it has Picard attempting to communicate with an alien in a deeply referential language. It’s in my Top Four for all Trek.

 5x05 Disaster. The ship is damaged with the crew in uncomfortable positions: Troi in command, Worf asked to deliver a baby, and Picard in charge of a children’s tour.

 5x16 Ethics. Worf is injured and must cope with what he sees as a dishonourable state, while Crusher butts head with a researcher on an experimental treatment for him.

 5x23 I, Borg. The Borg are relentless and amoral, a cybernetic species bent on assimilating every sentient they find into their Collective. Picard has an opportunity to destroy them using a single Borg drone who has achieved independent thought.

 5x25 The Inner Light. One of my Top Four favourite Trek episodes of all time. Trek is ultimately not about fancy gizmos, unlikely science or even space, but what it means to be a sentient being, to have lived a life and left a legacy.

 6x09 The Quality of Life. Data finds himself able to argue for the rights of an emergent sentience, but he must risk Picard’s life to do so.

 6x10 & x11 Chain of Command. [Trigger alert: Scenes of torture.] Picard is sent on a covert mission deep into Cardassian space, and captured.

 6x12 Ship in a Bottle. Moriarty re-emerges and does the impossible: he walks off the Holodeck.

 6x15 Tapestry. Another of my Top Four. Picard was brash, hasty, and stupid when he was young (in other words, he was young) He is given a second chance, with consequences that are unbearable.

 6x19 Lessons. Office romances are a bad idea even in the 24th century. In space.

 6x25 Timescape. A beautiful piece of science fiction. Picard, Troi, Geordi and Data return to the ship to find it frozen in time, seemingly in the middle of an altercation with a Romulan ship. Picard draws a smiley face on a vapour cloud from a warp core breach.

 6x26 and 7x01 Descent. Lore returns and hacks Data to try and invade the Federation with a posse of Borg.

 7x07 Dark Page. Deanna explores a repressed chapter of her mother’s past.

 7x25 & 26 All Good Things.... Picard finds himself shifting involunarily between three very different timelines, and Q helps him save the universe. The last episode of TNG.

## Deep Space 9

These are relatively stand-alone, and give you a small taste of what made DS9 different and great. If after watching Duet and Necessary Evil, you are really liking what you are seeing, I recommended looking up a "Dominion War Arc" watch-guide, and watching all of those episodes. I believe this is most of seasons 4 and 5, but the Dominion and Changelings were mentioned in earlier episodes too.

1x01-03 Sets the introductory context and central tension for the show.

1x09 Duet. Considered the best episode in all of Star Trek.

2x08 Necessary Evil. Another dark episode about war. Featuring O'Brien, my favourite dad.

4x05 Rejoined. First televised lesbian kiss!

4x19 Hard Time. Hopefully by now you've seen how much more deeply DS9 goes into themes of war.

5x21 Soldiers of the Empire. I would be remiss to not include a klingon-focused episode. They are, after the all, the Shakespeare characters of Star Trek.

6x18 Inquisition. I love Bashir! I needed to add something.
